Privacy

Ash Proxy is a self-hosted project based on the Nebula template. This deployment is operated independently from Nebula Services. Its operator controls the server, configuration, and any logging or data retention. Do not enter sensitive information unless you trust the operator.

When you browse through the proxy, requests are routed through this server to the sites you choose. The proxy software may process connection details needed to handle those requests. This project does not promise that traffic is anonymous or that the operator keeps no logs. Ask the deployment operator about their practices.

Browser preferences are stored locally in your browser. Third-party sites opened through the proxy have their own privacy policies and may set their own cookies.
