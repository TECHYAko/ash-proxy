Privacy

Ash Proxy does not load Google Analytics. Search terms entered without a website address are sent to DuckDuckGo through the proxy.

Browser storage

The site uses browser storage for preferences such as themes and tab cloaking. This information is kept in your browser. Games and third-party sites opened through the proxy may store their own data.

Proxied websites

When you browse a site, Ash Proxy requests that site on your behalf. The destination site may receive the request details it needs to serve the page, and its own privacy policy applies.
